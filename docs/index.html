01 Drawings

Dimensions in mm · proposed geometry

Main deck & rebuilt strip

Two double-bearer rows at Y350 / 2250, with six piles per row. Four joists continue into the rebuilt strip, supported by two new 900 mm doubled bearers at Y−800 / −1795. No capacity is assumed for the unknown retained bearer.

Use continuous 6600 mm bearer plies cut from 7200 mm stock. This removes both former bearer splice locations. Nineteen base joist lines, maximum 380 mm centres, plus two board-joint sisters.

Tread: one ordinary box frame

Two doubled 140 × 45 rails and 21 flush crosspieces, with 32 mm pine boards running along the frontage. Minimum H4 SG8 framing; graded H5 is an available supply route. Seven proposed crosswise concrete supports bear under both rails.

No short timber piles under the tread. DPC separates timber and concrete; stainless connections are intended to restrain sideways movement and uplift. Their complete capacity and footing detail still need checking.

The low support now fits the selected height.

250 − 32 − 140 = 78 mm beneath the timber. With 2 mm DPC, concrete bearing tops are nominally +76 mm. No blanket ground lowering is assumed. Keep drainage and the opening below the plain front rail; buried foundations may extend beneath adjacent landscaping.

Board layout and ordinary framing fixings

80 mm board / 5 mm gap example: main 31 rows with two 65 mm edge rips; rebuilt 11 rows with two 65 mm edge rips; tread five rows with two 70 mm edge rips. Actual width/moisture and supplier instructions govern the gap. Support each end at the tread X3300 board seam.

Assemble tread crosspieces to the inner 45 mm rail plies before adding outer plies. Basis: three 100 × 3.75 mm stainless hand-driven end nails at each crosspiece end, 126 base nails. Through one ply, nominal penetration is 55 mm; through both it would be only 10 mm.

Built-up-member basis: 90 × 4 mm stainless nails, two rows at maximum 250 mm centres along each row, alternating driving faces. Check actual nail geometry, splitting/edge distances and clashes. Ordinary joist/bearer and rim/joist basis: two 100 × 3.75 hand-driven nails at each applicable joint; rated anchor and uplift joints require their complete detail instead.

Main footings and low pile-top processing

Conditional good-ground proposal: six 350-square × 900-deep anchor holes and ten 350-square × 450-deep ordinary holes, with illustrative 800/350 mm timber embedments and 100 mm concrete below. Net 1.083063 m³ before crowns/waste. These are planning dimensions, not a verified footing selection for this site.

Nominal main pile top is +186 with 2 mm DPC. Pinepac documents 150 mm minimum processing height and drying/preservative treatment. All cuts and surface entries in the complete connection must satisfy that rule, not just the pile top. Other brands may differ. No blanket excavation depth is selected.

04 What remains before building

No new owner preference questions

  1. 1 · Main anchor / uplift detailBuilder/designer: complete six low pile-to-double-bearer-to-joist anchor assemblies and overall bracing transfer. Confirm the lowest pile cuts/fixings, both-ply load transfer and rated hold-downs for rebuilt continuous joists.
  2. 2 · Tread pad / anchor detailDesigner or manufacturer-supported design: final concrete foundation dimensions, any reinforcement, matched stainless angle/bolt/concrete-anchor assembly and capacities for lateral movement, uplift and overturning. The PBB8515/SS drawing is a fit candidate, not a rated assembly.
  3. 3 · Ground, levels & drainagePre-build site check: establish bearing ground, finished path/garden levels, drainage and existing-foundation conflicts. Keep landscaping clear of timber. Buried foundations can extend beyond the visible 400 mm tread.
  4. 4 · Actual products & quoteMerchant: confirm long graded/treatment-marked stock, pile instructions, actual pine-board width/moisture/profile, compatible fixings and delivered prices. The owner has already said the exact board product is unknown.

Selected heights are preserved. Access compliance remains unresolved: the nominal 250 mm rises exceed the D1/AS1 Table 6 limits (maximum 220 mm across its stair categories), so the applicable compliance approach still needs settling before building. This is separate from the structural connections; no height change is assumed here.

One pre-build discussion, with any necessary product/designer input completed before construction. If actual ground or dimensions differ from the agreed plan, resolve the affected detail before continuing.
