Before building

  • Confirm four corner anchors, actual anchor-pile grade/section, low connection clearance and full bracing/load path. No blanket ground lowering is selected.
  • Complete the front-rim/cleat/bolt and tread board/crosspiece/end-joint/outer-pad restraint checks. Inner-rail preliminary support reaction reaches 5.299 kN; rim eccentric moment 0.477 kNm.
  • Check house cladding clearance, door-threshold height and weatherproofing/drainage against the actual door/cladding detail. Do not apply one universal gap to all claddings or doors.
  • Confirm H4 SG8 lengths and compatible fasteners. Keep the builder review before work; later checks are by the owner.

Build sequence

  1. Check the house cladding gap, door threshold/weatherproofing detail and actual 500/250 mm datums with the builder; complete the hanging and anchor details.
  2. Mark all footprints, sixteen timber-pile positions and six outer-rail pad positions from Drawing 1. Keep surrounding ground levels.
  3. Dig the footing holes to the agreed bearing and depth schedule; set piles and individual pad forms.
  4. Set all six pad bearing tops level at nominal+77.25 mm, place the specified concrete and observe cure/anchor installation requirements.
  5. Fit continuous main double bearers at Y300/2510 and the two 900 mm rebuilt double bearers. Replace the old deck bearer safely; do not assume it supports only the deck without checking.
  6. Fit seventeen main joist members and three separate rebuilt joists. Fit the two lateral blocks over the intermediate rebuilt bearer and the rear hold-downs.
  7. Build the front rim: continuous inner ply plus the seven vertical cleat heads and eight outer infill pieces. Keep the cleats clear of joist ends and bolt access.
  8. Bolt the tread inner rail to the cleats to the completed fixing detail. Assemble seventeen 310 mm H4 crosspieces and the continuous outer rail.
  9. Seat the outer rail on DPC at the six pad tops. Complete the agreed positive restraint and main bracing/load path before boards.
  10. Lay 32 mm pine boards along X in the main deck, rebuilt strip and tread. Support butt ends on paired members and use supplier-approved gaps/screws.
  11. Treat permitted cuts and finish the lawn/path/garden clear of timber. Check the owner-built work against the agreed plan; no builder inspection visits are assumed.
