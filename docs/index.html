To discuss in person

The details to work through

  • Exact decking product & finish

    38 mm-thick pine is selected. Confirm actual board width, finish, wet/dry gap guidance and stock lengths.

  • Vertical access detail

    The selected halfway tread is 250 mm above garden, with two approximately 250 mm rises. Acceptable access geometry and any level adjustment need checking.

  • Rebuilt framing junction

    Joists reaching the rear bearer enter the main deck. Their connection, bearing and combined loads need a coherent detail.

  • Foundations and exact products

    Confirm bracing, footing geometry, exact connectors, ground conditions and support capacity.
