01 Drawings

Dimensions in mm · support layout is provisional

Main deck

Double 140 × 45 H3.2 SG8 bearers and 140 × 45 SG8 joists. Continuous 6600 mm bearer plies cut from 7200 mm stock. Four joists continue through the rebuilt strip, with new rear support.

Simple box tread

Two doubled 140 × 45 rails carry short 400 mm pine boards front-to-back. Nineteen lower 90 × 45 crosspieces brace the box. Minimum H4 SG8 framing; H5 is the suggested supply route. DPC separates the rails from concrete.
