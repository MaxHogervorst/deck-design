# Deck project instructions

Use CURRENT_DESIGN.md as the single active design document. design.json and the shopping CSV are generated by scripts/check_revision.py and scripts/render_plan.py. Do not keep a second copy of the quantities.

Current owner direction (11 November 2026): the owner is building this themselves. No consent (under 1.5 m) and no engineer sign-off. A builder walked the site on 11 November and the holes, the concrete and the steps follow that conversation. It is still not a sign-off. Every deck hole is 400×400×600. No 900 mm anchor piles and no 12 kN kits. The step is two rises of about 167 mm (three rises, going 280, 560 mm out from the face), a ribbon on the front piles and a front beam on five 300×300×400 piles, then the top step built on that. Not a cut stringer and not the five paver frames. The bottom beam is full depth. Do not rip it. Where it meets the path, chase the concrete. No spare pile beside the path. No handrail. ROBO preview ISL38807 is not the build and its bill of materials is only a layout example. Keep the deck level with the old 500 mm deck. The concrete path stays. Waste and stormwater are a pair on the west side of the path. The blue pipe is 400 mm past the concrete and the sewage is 130 mm past that (870 mm off the deck minus 740 mm). Line 2 moves 200 mm onto the grass. Line 3 is 1,230 mm past the concrete. Main bearers are doubled 190×45 for the whole deck, each ply butted on the pile at 3067 so the piece is continuous over two spans. NZS 3604:2011 Table 6.4(b) allows 2.7 m. The pile line at 3947.5 is not used. Bays are 615, 2390, 2175 and 1295 mm. Concrete is a 2.0 m³ small-load truck, or builders mix plus cement at four shovels to one, a hole at a time. Not no-mix post-hole bags. The shopping list is a supplier comparison dated 11 November 2026. The total uses the truck, not the cement as well. The A2 set is three sheets, drawn to AS/NZS 1100.501 and the Auckland Council AC1011 list, and it is not lodged. Do not invent a boundary, a surveyed level, or a north point.

Current geometry uses main bearers Y300/2510; span 2.21 m is below the 2.35 m wet-service SG8 limit at 450 centres. Ten deck piles, five on each bearer, plus five step piles. The rebuilt strip is 900×1970 with no new piles. Its three joists are 2270 mm, set 45 mm beside the deck joists, and run from the house ledger to the first new bearer so the lap can be nailed. The long span is 1.97 m, under the 2.35 m in Table 7.1(b). The main deck is not carried by the house. Framing stock is 7.2, 6.0 and 4.8 m to match what Bunnings and Mitre 10 list. Shopping list prices are Bunnings and Mitre 10 where a figure is shown (11 November 2026), split into posted, estimate and alternatives that are not in the total. Trade quotes come later.

The published page is the three A2 sheets, then the bill of materials and the fixing schedule. No build-order essay on the site. The working notes stay in CURRENT_DESIGN.md.

Check actual applicable standard/manufacturer details; do not present the 600 mm holes or the ordinary screws as approved engineering. Spans are read from NZS 3604:2011 Table 6.4(b) and Table 7.1(b). Component demand screens are not full assembly verification. The removed old hold label is historical; physical connection checks remain. Preserve source evidence, private data and old work.
