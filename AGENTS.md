# Deck project instructions

Use CURRENT_DESIGN.md as the single active design document. BUILD_STEPS.md, OPEN_ISSUES.md, MATERIALS.md and SHOPPING_LIST.md point to its sections; do not duplicate quantities. design.json and the shopping CSV are generated by scripts/check_revision.py and scripts/render_plan.py. Historical research and previous plans live in archive/ and do not override current owner instructions.

Current owner direction (10 November 2026): the owner is building this themselves, with no council consent (under 1.5 m) and no builder or engineer sign-off. The website is a plain build guide written for the owner, not a builder brief. The step is five separate 900 mm frames of double 140×45 H4, centred at X710/2005/3300/4595/5890, each with three 220 mm crosspieces and one 250 mm tie. Each frame sits on four 300×300 pavers (220 mm each side of the frame centre, Y2645 and Y2955) in one 760×800 GAP7 pit in the middle of the bay, clear of the pile holes. The tie is a hold-down: four 14g×100 screws into the bearer and two into the inner rail. Packers are H4 offcuts screwed to the rail, not plastic. No continuous 6.6 m rail, no second concrete pour, no formwork, no anchors in the pits. The cast-pad plan is archived in archive/2026-11-09-cast-pad-tread-plan and the hanging tread in archive/2026-11-09-hanging-tread-plan. Front rim is a plain continuous 140×45 at Y2555–2600; joists cut 2510. No H5 tread special order. All boards along X: 28 deck rows, 21 rebuilt rows, 4 step boards cut to 6400 mm. Replace the old rebuilt bearer; no dependence on retained timber and no joists continuous through both deck areas. Joists: end joist flush, then 437 mm centres, one doubled joist centred at 3956 (16 base lines plus that pair is 17 members). Rebuilt blocks are cut at 380 mm. Four corner anchor piles with 12 kN kits are the owner's default, listed as an assumption, and the bracket has to fit a 90 mm double bearer. Keep the deck level with the old 500 mm deck. The concrete path stays and the deck spans it; no pile hole through the concrete. Waste and rainwater run down the west side, about 700–900 mm off the deck, 240–550 mm deep: no hole within 500 mm, hand dig nearby, and do not open a bearer bay past 1.4 m. Ordinary footing holes only, square or a 400 mm auger clear of those services. Mini-mix is the pour; 115 bags of Multicrete, mixed, is only the fallback. Measure sill, cladding and the path clearance before buying.

Current geometry uses main bearers Y300/2510; span 2.21 m is below the 2.35 m wet-service SG8 limit at 450 centres. Rebuilt rear face Y−1970 means bearer centre −1925; other row −800. Three separate rebuilt joists and two lateral blocks remain. Framing stock is 7.2, 6.0 and 4.8 m to match what Bunnings and Mitre 10 list. Shopping list prices are Bunnings/Mitre 10 web prices (read 10 November 2026) split into posted and estimate; keep that split and that baseline, trade quotes come later.

Keep the site one page with three drawings: foundations/heights, framing/sizes, fixing schedule. Include the shopping list, cut list and build order. The cladding-clearance, door-threshold and beforeUdig checks are step 1 of the build order; everything unverified is a plain-language bullet in the closing "Assumptions" section, written in the owner's voice ("this is what I would do; ideally an engineer would check"). No builder brief, builder visits, photos or paid-designer appointment are assumed. Keep heights/no-handrail preference, without claiming access compliance.

Check actual applicable standard/manufacturer details; do not present the corner anchors, the paver pits or the step ties as approved. Component demand screens are not full assembly verification. The removed old hold label is historical; physical connection checks remain. Preserve source evidence, private data and old work. No unsolicited PDFs or supplier messages. User authorised website changes/publication; publication is not construction approval.
