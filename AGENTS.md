# Deck project instructions

Use CURRENT_DESIGN.md as the single active design document. BUILD_STEPS.md, OPEN_ISSUES.md, MATERIALS.md and SHOPPING_LIST.md point to its sections; do not duplicate quantities. design.json and the shopping CSV are generated by scripts/check_revision.py and scripts/render_plan.py. Historical research and previous plans live in archive/ and do not override current owner instructions.

Current owner direction (10 November 2026): the owner is building this themselves, with no council consent (under 1.5 m) and no builder or engineer sign-off. The website is a plain build guide written for the owner, not a builder brief. The step is a ladder frame of two double 140×45 H4 SG8 rails (90 wide, Y2600–2690 and Y2910–3000) with 17 crosspieces of 220, bearing on six pads at X420/1765/3060/3540/4835/6180; each pad is two 300×300 concrete pavers (centred Y2645 and Y2955) on compacted GAP7 in a 450×800 pit dug to firm ground, clear of every pile hole. Rails sit free on DPC and plastic packers; three 250 mm H4 cleats on the front bearer face tie the step sideways and carry no weight. No second concrete pour, no formwork, no anchors in the pads. The cast-pad plan is archived in archive/2026-11-09-cast-pad-tread-plan and the hanging tread in archive/2026-11-09-hanging-tread-plan. Front rim is a plain continuous 140×45 at Y2555–2600; joists cut 2510. No H5 tread special order. All boards along X. Replace the old rebuilt bearer; no dependence on retained timber and no joists continuous through both deck areas. Thin main joists to 450 maximum centres, but count correctly: 16 base lines plus a supported butt pair gives 17 main members. Four corner anchor piles with 12 kN kits are the owner's default, listed as an assumption. Keep 500/250 levels and surrounding ground; ordinary footing holes only.

Current geometry uses main bearers Y300/2510; span 2.21 m is below the 2.35 m wet-service SG8 limit at 450 centres. Rebuilt rear face Y−1970 means bearer centre −1925; other row −800. Three separate rebuilt joists and two lateral blocks remain. Framing stock is 7.2, 6.0 and 4.8 m to match what Bunnings and Mitre 10 list. Shopping list prices are Bunnings/Mitre 10 web prices (read 10 November 2026) split into posted and estimate; keep that split and that baseline, trade quotes come later.

Keep the site one page with three drawings: foundations/heights, framing/sizes, fixing schedule. Include the shopping list, cut list and build order. The cladding-clearance, door-threshold and beforeUdig checks are step 1 of the build order; everything unverified is a plain-language bullet in the closing "Assumptions" section, written in the owner's voice ("this is what I would do; ideally an engineer would check"). No builder brief, builder visits, photos or paid-designer appointment are assumed. Keep heights/no-handrail preference, without claiming access compliance.

Check actual applicable standard/manufacturer details; do not present the corner anchors, the paver pads or the cleats as approved. Component demand screens are not full assembly verification. The removed old hold label is historical; physical connection checks remain. Preserve source evidence, private data and old work. No unsolicited PDFs or supplier messages. User authorised website changes/publication; publication is not construction approval.
