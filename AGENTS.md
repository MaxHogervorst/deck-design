# Deck project instructions

Use CURRENT_DESIGN.md as the single active design document. BUILD_STEPS.md, OPEN_ISSUES.md, MATERIALS.md and SHOPPING_LIST.md point to its sections; do not duplicate quantities. design.json and the shopping CSV are generated by scripts/check_revision.py and scripts/render_plan.py. Historical research and previous plans live in archive/ and do not override current owner instructions.

Current owner direction (11 November 2026): the owner is building this themselves. No consent (under 1.5 m) and no engineer sign-off. A builder walked the site on 11 November and the holes, the concrete and the steps follow that conversation. It is still not a sign-off. Every deck hole is 400×400×600. No 900 mm anchor piles and no 12 kN kits. The step is two rises of about 167 mm (three rises, going 280, 560 mm out from the face), a ribbon screwed to the front piles and a front beam on six 300×300×400 piles, then the top step built on that. Not a cut stringer and not the five paver frames. The bottom beam is ripped where it would hit the path, minimum 120 mm, with a spare pile each side of the path. No handrail. ROBO preview ISL38807 is not the build and its bill of materials is only a layout example. Keep the deck level with the old 500 mm deck. The concrete path stays. Waste and stormwater run down the west side: no hole within 500 mm, dig with a spade, do not open a bearer bay past 1.4 m. Concrete is a 2.0 m³ small-load truck, or builders mix plus cement at four shovels to one, a hole at a time. Not no-mix post-hole bags. The shopping list is a supplier comparison dated 11 November 2026. The total uses the truck, not the cement as well. The A2 set is three sheets, drawn to AS/NZS 1100.501 and the Auckland Council AC1011 list, and it is not lodged. Do not invent a boundary, a surveyed level, or a north point.

Current geometry uses main bearers Y300/2510; span 2.21 m is below the 2.35 m wet-service SG8 limit at 450 centres. Rebuilt rear face Y−1970 means bearer centre −1925; other row −800. Three separate rebuilt joists and two lateral blocks remain. Framing stock is 7.2, 6.0 and 4.8 m to match what Bunnings and Mitre 10 list. Shopping list prices are Bunnings and Mitre 10 where a figure is shown (11 November 2026), split into posted, estimate and alternatives that are not in the total. Trade quotes come later.

Keep the site one page with three drawings: foundations/heights, framing/sizes, fixing schedule. Include the shopping list, cut list and build order. The cladding-clearance, door-threshold and beforeUdig checks are step 1 of the build order; everything unverified is a plain-language bullet in the closing "Assumptions" section, written in the owner's voice ("this is what I would do; ideally an engineer would check"). No builder brief, builder visits, photos or paid-designer appointment are assumed. Keep heights/no-handrail preference, without claiming access compliance.

Check actual applicable standard/manufacturer details; do not present the 600 mm holes, the ripped step beam or the ordinary screws as approved engineering. Component demand screens are not full assembly verification. The removed old hold label is historical; physical connection checks remain. Preserve source evidence, private data and old work. No unsolicited PDFs or supplier messages. User authorised website changes/publication; publication is not construction approval.
