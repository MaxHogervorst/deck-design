# Deck project instructions

Use CURRENT_DESIGN.md as the single active design document. design.json and the shopping CSV are generated by scripts/check_revision.py and scripts/render_plan.py. Do not keep a second copy of the quantities.

Current owner direction (13 November 2026): eight deck piles, four on each bearer, at 300, 2400, 4500 and 6300 mm from the eastern end. The bearer overhangs 300 mm past each end pile. Bays are 2100, 2100 and 1800 mm. Bearers are doubled 190×70 SG8 H3.2, butted on the pile at 4500. NZS 3604 Table 6.4(b) allows one 190×70 to span 2.7 m. The same standard caps that overhang at 200 mm when the bearers are more than 2 m apart. The 300 mm is the builder's set-out. Mark the holes and move one if it lands on the path or within 500 mm of a pipe. The four step piles are on the same lines and are dug later. No 900 mm anchor piles and no 12 kN kits. The step is two rises of about 167 mm (three rises, going 280, 560 mm out from the face). Not a cut stringer. The bottom beam is full depth. Do not rip it. Where it meets the path, chase the concrete. No handrail. Keep the deck level with the old 500 mm deck. The concrete path stays. Concrete for the eight holes is about 0.77 m³. A truck minimum is often 2.0 m³. Ask the smallest load and the fee to take the rest back. Not no-mix post-hole bags.

Current geometry uses main bearers Y300/2510; span 2.21 m is below the 2.35 m wet-service SG8 limit at 450 centres. Eight deck piles, four on each bearer, plus four step piles later. The rebuilt strip is 900×1970 with no new piles. Its three joists are 2270 mm, set 45 mm beside the deck joists, and run from the house ledger to the first new bearer so the lap can be nailed. The long span is 1.97 m, under the 2.35 m in Table 7.1(b). The main deck is not carried by the house.

The published page is the three A2 sheets, then the bill of materials and the fixing schedule. No build-order essay on the site. The working notes stay in CURRENT_DESIGN.md.

Check actual applicable standard/manufacturer details; do not present the 600 mm holes or the ordinary screws as approved engineering. Spans are read from NZS 3604:2011 Table 6.4(b) and Table 7.1(b). Component demand screens are not full assembly verification. The removed old hold label is historical; physical connection checks remain. Preserve source evidence, private data and old work.
