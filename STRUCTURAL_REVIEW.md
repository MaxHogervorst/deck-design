## Bracing calculation and connections

Area excluding independent tread =17.16+1.773=18.933 m².

Published BRANZ Table5.8 excerpt gives zone1 /soil classD&E multiplier0.5. The freestanding-deck method uses7.5 BU/m² before this factor:

`18.933 ×7.5 ×0.5 =70.99875 ≈71 BU in each direction`.

Proposed main anchors:P01/P03/P06 on rear row andP07/P09/P12 on front row, atX250/2690/6350; other six main piles ordinary. AcrossX, bracing-line gaps2440/3660, both below5 m; Y row separation1900. A120 BU anchor rating is not a complete bracing design: confirm each direction's rated load path, line/perimeter distribution, retained-strip transfer, connection geometry and actual applicable standard.

Select a complete stainless12 kN system, not an assortment of angles. MiTek12KNH is the current candidate; its plates/cleats/skew nails must all be installed to its applicable detail. Its illustrated plate extends80 mm onto the pile; nominal188 mm pile exposure provides geometric space, but this does not settle the90 mm built-up bearer connection, edge distances or all fasteners. PrydaPBK12S is an alternative, with explicit bearer-width/application conditions and a different plate/pile detail. Do not mix the kits or copy its document's apparent “cantilever” labelling error as permission to use a6 kN connection at an anchor.

Main bearer butt splices proposed over ordinary piles atX3910. Segments3.910+2.690 m:4×4.2 +4×3.0 m stock for four main plies =28.8 lm before kerf (26.4 installed). Do not splice over the six proposed main anchors. Complete ordinary-pile and joist/bearer connections still require the actual fastening schedule.

For rebuilt block ends, a stainless45 mm hanger such as PrydaMPFB45120/S is a **candidate only**. Pryda's published capacity table assumes dry timber, so it is not a wet-service capacity proof. Have the matching wet-service rating and exact connector fasteners confirmed. Apply a structural fixing schedule to main lateral blocks as well.

## Retained bearer — what can and cannot be established

The1970 mm value is already the confirmed rebuilt depth. The latest reply also calls1970 a spacing and comments on the board axis; it could refer to supports, but its endpoints/direction are not clear. It is not treated as a verified distance between the retained beam’s own piles acrossX. The beam width/depth is still unreported. Those, its grade/treatment, decay, connections and foundations are unknown. No capacity or condition conclusion is possible from that measurement alone.

The intermediate bearer reduces a simple tributary indication at the retained support to roughly0.9×1.17/2=0.5265 m², before continuity effects and concentrated loads. That is not a strength check. Reuse remains conditional on measured section/supports and condition. If it cannot be established, replace its support line with a new reviewed bearer/pile detail; that replacement is excluded from the quantities below. No claim of approval is made merely because the tributary load is small.
