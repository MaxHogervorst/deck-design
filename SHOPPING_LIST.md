## Decking length mix

For the proposed88/90mm product,270 lm is a price allowance, not a demand for arbitrary short pieces. At illustrative gaps the calculated stock scenarios are41×4.2m +28×3.0m =256.2 lm at88mm, or40×4.2m +28×3.0m =252.0 lm at90mm, before an extra defect allowance. A 4.2m board supplies two1970mm rebuilt pieces or ten400mm tread pieces. Main rows use a supported joint nearX3895. The random-length merchant must confirm an equivalent usable mix and any selected-length surcharge. Actual width/moisture/gap rules govern before cutting.
