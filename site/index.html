Anchor-pile fixings

Plan input AP01

MiTek is optional. Specify the complete connection required by the bracing plan. MiTek’s 12 kN pack is for anchor/brace piles; its 6 kN pack is for cantilever piles.

For this low MiTek connection: the 2024 brochure specifies stainless 12KNH for fixings below 600 mm above ground. Its detail does not explicitly verify the doubled 140 × 45 bearers or fit at the nominal 182 mm pile exposure. Confirm AP01 before ordering; the previous four-anchor idea is not an approved allocation.

MiTek 12 kN installation detail, 2024 V1, pp. 45–46 · Use the full specified plate/cleat/nail assembly. Do not substitute a generic bracket or assume every pile is an anchor.
