Tread supports: no concrete

Recommended alternative · GP01
  1. Test-drive one fully assembled500 mm Nuraspike with its hitting plate. Suitable soil must let it reach flush with ground and hold firmly.
  2. Install seven spikes under each rail at X150/1200/2250/3300/4350/5400/6450. Proposed spacing1050 mm, end overhang150 mm; confirm against the selected140×45 rail and supplier spacing requirements.
  3. Place a Nurajack with timber head on each spike. Set rail undersides to78 mm above the garden datum using the screw adjustment; check every support’s actual gap.
  4. Fix the timber to the heads with the manufacturer’s30 mm10-gauge stainless screws. Install the agreed lateral restraint before boards.

The75–120 mm NJSE03-J is a candidate only:78 mm is close to its minimum and the spike top affects the available height. Select the head range from the actual spike-top-to-rail measurement. Do not put ordinary pedestal bases directly on soil. Nuraspikes are unsuitable for waterlogged, heavily compacted or rocky soil. The system needs lateral restraint; the narrow independent tread’s restraint and any uplift requirement remain to design.

Anchor-pile fixings

Plan input AP01

MiTek is optional. Specify the complete connection required by the bracing plan. MiTek’s 12 kN pack is for anchor/brace piles; its 6 kN pack is for cantilever piles.

For this low MiTek connection: the 2024 brochure specifies stainless 12KNH for fixings below 600 mm above ground. Its detail does not explicitly verify the doubled 140 × 45 bearers or fit at the nominal 188 mm pile exposure. Confirm AP01 before ordering; six main anchor locations are now proposed, subject to the complete connection/bracing review.
