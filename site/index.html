01 Drawings

Dimensions in mm · proposed geometry

Main deck & rebuilt strip

Two double-bearer rows at Y350 / 2250, with eight piles per row. Four joists continue into the rebuilt strip, supported by two new 900 mm doubled bearers at Y−800 / −1795. No capacity is assumed for the unknown retained bearer.

Use continuous 6600 mm bearer plies cut from 7200 mm stock. This removes both former bearer splice locations. Nineteen base joist lines, maximum 380 mm centres, plus two board-joint sisters.

Tread: one ordinary box frame

Two doubled 140 × 45 rails carry short 400 mm pine boards running front-to-back. Nineteen lower 90 × 45 crosspieces brace the box, 50 mm below the boards. Minimum H4 SG8 framing; graded H5 is an available supply route. Seven proposed concrete pads bear under both rails; no walking-load hangers are needed inside the box.

No short timber piles under the tread. DPC separates timber and concrete; stainless connections are intended to restrain sideways movement and uplift. Their complete capacity and footing detail still need checking.

The low support now fits the selected height.

250 − 32 − 140 = 78 mm beneath the tread timber. With 0.75 mm DPC, concrete tops are nominally +77.25 mm. No tread-ground lowering is proposed. The separate main anchor detail proposes 100 mm of soil lowering under the deck, conditional on drainage; see the explanation below.

04 Three things to finish

No more owner preference questions

  1. 1 · Complete connection drawingTake the pre-build connection checklist to the builder. The deliverable is one marked-up foundation/fixing drawing: complete six 12KNH joints, both bearer plies, actual cuts/nail zones, overall bracing, rebuilt hold-downs and the tread assembly. Manufacturer/designer input is needed where the detail has no published assembly rating. You do not need to calculate this yourself.
  2. 2 · Soil clearance and drainageThe 100 mm proposal lowers soil under the main deck, keeping the deck, tread and surrounding lawn at their chosen heights. It gives the selected six anchor heads enough cut clearance. Flat grass does not identify a drainage outlet. A person on site can measure the formation/outlet levels; no photo is needed. Do not dig a basin that holds water.
  3. 3 · Merchant quotesThe shopping list is ready for quotations: quantities, exact timber-length basis, supplier links, hardware and design holds. Confirm usable long stock, actual board dimensions/moisture and compatible screws, then compare delivered totals. No supplier has been contacted and no purchase made.
Does the ground really need to be 100 mm lower?

For the selected main anchor-head example, pile top is +187.25 and the lowest processed surface is +67.25. The selected pile instructions require 150 mm from soil to processed timber. Soil at −100 gives 167.25 mm clearance. The proposal comes from those six anchor details; it is not a rule for every low deck or for the tread.

The draft shows a lowered formation under the main/rebuilt deck. Before accepting it, measure whether water can reach a lawful lower outlet with positive fall. If there is no outlet, revise the low anchor/ground detail before excavation. Gravel and weed mat do not establish drainage. Plain-language levels and on-site steps.

Exactly what should the connection review return?

One annotated drawing and fastener schedule confirming the main load path and bracing in both directions, complete manufacturer kit fit, all nail/cut/ground-clearance limits, the genuine SSCPC40 screw pattern, and the full tread bracket/bolt/anchor/pad capacity. The tread uses standard bought components, but Pryda does not publish a rating for this assembled arrangement. The preliminary component screens do not replace that check.

The builder can confirm details within published standard instructions; departures and the un-rated tread assembly need a suitably qualified NZ designer or manufacturer-supported design. Complete this before construction, within the single pre-build review process; no builder inspection visits are added.

Owner reports flat normal ground and successful previous extension piles: 100 kPa allowable bearing is used as a planning assumption. Selected heights and no-handrail preference remain. Nominal 250 mm rises exceed the D1/AS1 Table 6 stair limits; an applicable access compliance approach remains necessary before building. Publication does not establish structural or access compliance.
