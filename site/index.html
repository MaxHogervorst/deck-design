Current development brief and unresolved fixing checks

Low post tops: treatment and footing design

Main post-top stack arithmetic is 500 − 32 − 140 − 140 = 188 mm before separation. The proposed 90 mm tread framing has its underside at 128 mm, before the nominal 2 mm DPC below it. The selected bearing arrangement and ground levels must provide the required clearance. Do not simply cut standard piles at these heights.

Pinepac’s house-pile instructions provide a 150 mm minimum processing height and require drying/preservative treatment of processed surfaces. Other brands can be stricter. Specify the actual supplied product and complete low-pile detail; H5 treatment alone does not establish structural grade.

The developed proposal specifies structural 125 × 125 H5 piles, with six candidate main anchors and ten ordinary deck/rebuilt posts, and proposes 20 MPa concrete. Final footing selection, bracing and post lengths still need the actual ground and complete connection detail. Manufacturer member-table comparisons have been checked; full current NZS 3604 project verification remains open.

04 Pre-build builder discussion

Resolve once, before construction

  1. FP01 · Posts & footingsSoil basis, structural H5 grade/size/count, pile classes, hole dimensions, concrete and actual post lengths.
  2. AP01 · Main framing & bracingWet-service spans and projections; double-bearer fixings; supported splices; full rated connections and restraint in both directions.
  3. GP01 · Narrow treadShort boards on shallow H5 bearers and staggered H5 piles. Check 50 mm board end projections, direct bearing/fixings, ordinary footing layout entirely inside 400 mm, deck ties, ground clearance and drainage.
  4. RB01 · New rebuilt rear supportDevelop the new Y−1795 rear bearer and two H5 posts. Check existing foundation conflicts and continuous-joist junctions; no retained timber capacity is assumed.
  5. DF01 · Decking & accessActual board/gap, supported joints, screws and edge distances. The nominal 250 mm rises exceed every stair-category limit in D1/AS1 Table 6 (maximum 220 mm). Preserve the selected levels in the proposal, but resolve the applicable compliance approach before building.

If actual soil or dimensions differ, stop the affected work and resolve the deviation before continuing. No builder inspection service is assumed.
