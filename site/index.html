Low post tops: treatment and footing design

Main post-top stack arithmetic is 500 − 32 − 140 − 140 = 188 mm before separation. The tread head makes its post top lower than 78 mm. Do not simply cut standard piles at these heights.

The Pinex January 2026 guide, §1.3 prohibits its post-treatment cut ends in ground or within 300 mm of ground. Obtain a manufacturer-approved treatment and processing detail satisfying the applicable requirements. H5 treatment alone does not establish structural grade.

Footing dimensions and post lengths remain open. Confirm soil bearing, pile classes, concrete beneath posts, embedment and complete bracing load paths against the applicable standard. Current full NZS 3604 tables have not been verified for this project.

04 Pre-build builder discussion

Resolve once, before construction

  1. FP01 · Posts & footingsSoil basis, structural H5 grade/size/count, pile classes, hole dimensions, concrete and actual post lengths.
  2. AP01 · Main framing & bracingWet-service spans and projections; double-bearer fixings; supported splices; full rated connections and restraint in both directions.
  3. GP01 · Narrow treadFootings entirely inside 400 mm; designed transverse heads, both rail bearings, low-height durability and resistance to lateral movement, uplift and rotation.
  4. RB01 · Retained supportMeasured member, own supports, condition and load capacity, including the rebuilt-strip junction.
  5. DF01 · Decking & accessActual board/gap, supported joints, screws and edge distances. Selected 500/250 levels are not a verified access-compliance solution.

If actual soil or dimensions differ, stop the affected work and resolve the deviation before continuing. No builder inspection service is assumed.
