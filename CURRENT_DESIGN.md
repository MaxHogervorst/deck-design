# Current deck design — published development snapshot

8 November 2026, Auckland. **Pre-build discussion proposal, not a construction release.**

## Owner decisions

- Main 6600 × 2600 mm; rebuilt 900 × 1970 mm; one full-width tread 6600 × 400 mm.
- Surfaces about 500 / 250 mm above garden. Pine decking 32 mm thick, approximately 80 mm wide. Owner accepts **50 mm tread-board end overhang**; the earlier 15 mm message was corrected.
- Both deck and tread use structural H5 timber posts concreted into excavated holes. All tread excavation/concrete stays inside its 400 mm footprint.
- Main framing preference: double 140 × 45 SG8 bearers and 140 × 45 SG8 joists.
- Tread may connect to the freestanding deck for sideways restraint; its own posts carry its weight. No house support, no proposed handrails, one pre-build discussion and one owner sequence; no builder inspections during construction.

## Fixings — verification status at publication

Working board-fixing basis:75 mm316 stainless decking screws, two per board per support; actual gauge/head/pilot/edge rules need the actual product instructions.

Ordinary seated timber-pile connections have a four100×3.75 skew-nail route in NZS3604:2011 §6.5.2(b), or the complete MiTek OPF nail/wire-dog arrangement. Exposure, splices, clashes and uplift still need checking. These are not12 kN joints.

Main double140×45 ply-fastening development: two rows90 mm stainless structural nails,250 mm maximum per row, alternating driven faces; diameter and end/edge/joint details to verify. Tread two70 mm plies need at least52.5 mm penetration into the final ply:125 mm nails give55 nominal; a100 mm nail is too short. No exact tread structural nail SKU is released.

Six main anchors require complete rated pile→bearer→joist connections, both-ply transfer and the necessary lateral-support/blocking lines. MiTek12KNH is a candidate family; its fit and low-pile processing constraints are unresolved. Main supported bearer splices need their applicable splice detail separately.

Four vertical timber drop-cleats with through-bolted interfaces are a dimensional candidate for tread-to-deck restraint. Their fastener capacities, timber bending, rim/blocking load transfer and the combined bracing are **not yet verified**. Deck rim bottom+328 is110 mm above tread framing top+218, so a generic small hanger does not bridge the height gap.

## Remaining pre-build checks

1. Physical good ground, spot levels, draining ground profile and existing-foundation conflicts.
2. Qualified check of main anchor/splice/joist/rim/block joints and the tread/deck restraint detail.
3. Actual pine-board suitability for50 mm end projections, actual width/moisture/gaps and screw instructions. Owner acceptance of the dimension does not establish board capacity.
4. Supplied structural timber and pile manufacturer's processing instructions. Pinepac publishes a150 mm minimum processing height; other brands can be stricter.

Selected access levels remain in the proposal; this is not an access-compliance finding.
