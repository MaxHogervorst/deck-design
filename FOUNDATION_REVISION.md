# H5 posts in concrete — current builder discussion brief
8 November 2026 (Auckland). **Design development only; not a construction or purchasing schedule.**

## Confirmed by user
Both deck and tread use excavated holes with structural H5 timber posts/poles set in concrete. This decision supersedes every earlier foundation proposal. Main deck 6600 × 2600; rebuilt section 900 × 1970; one full-width tread 6600 × 400 mm. Selected deck/tread surfaces +500/+250 mm relative to garden. All tread foundations, including concrete and excavation, must fit inside its footprint. Pine decking is 32 mm thick, approximately 80 mm wide. Main framing preference is double 140 × 45 bearers and 140 × 45 SG8 joists. Freestanding; no handrails proposed. One pre-build builder discussion and one owner build sequence, without builder inspection visits. Website changes and publication each require express permission.

## 3. Tread: centred posts and transverse heads
**PROVISIONAL development route:** one centred row of structural H5 posts at Y2800. Each post carries a specifically designed transverse head supporting both long rails of a single-layer ladder frame. Gravity: boards → short crosspieces/rails → transverse heads → H5 posts → concrete → suitable soil. The head also needs a designed load path for sideways movement, uplift and rotation from loading either edge.

Seven stations give six bays of1016.7 mm and250 mm end projections. This derives **7 tread posts/heads**, subject to rail, head, post and footing checks.

Two candidate 140 × 45 rails have outer faces Y2600/3000, centres Y2622.5/2977.5. Clear width between rails310 mm. Propose crosspiece support centres no greater than400 mm, but verify the actual 32 mm decking product before releasing the layout. Rail joints must be directly supported by designed heads; arrange stock around the final head positions.

**Footprint envelope:** a centred350 ×350 mm square hole occupies Y2625…2975, leaving25 mm to each footprint edge. A400 mm circular hole leaves zero tolerance and is not the candidate here. This350 square is a spatial allowance, **not a verified footing specification**. Required soil bearing, cover, embedment, construction tolerance or lateral resistance may make it inadequate; the designer must resolve that within400 mm, not enlarge it outside the footprint. Excavation overbreak also must fit.

**Low-height connection is the critical unresolved detail.** The rail underside is only+78. A head of structural depth h below its bearing surface puts post top at roughly+78−h, before separation; it can approach or fall below garden. Do not invent a thin plate or bolt-only suspension to make the sketch fit. Establish head material, depth, bearing, corrosion protection, drainage, post exposure and assembly access as one detail. A low timber pile top is not automatically within the standard prescriptive detail. No standard anchor-pile bracing rating is assigned to this custom arrangement.

The head could be a fabricated connector only after specific design; no steel dimensions, bolts, notches or hole pattern are selected here. Main anchor kits cannot be assumed to rate this head. If the head/clearance cannot be resolved, revise the tread framing within the selected H5-in-concrete approach before proceeding.

**Verified with primary source:** CHH Pinex January2026 guide, section1.3, prohibits post-treatment cut ends in ground or within300 mm of ground, and requires approved preservative at processing. Its instructions apply to its products. Nominal main top+188 and tread top below+78 do not fit that cut-end rule. Brushing preservative alone does not remove the restriction. Obtain correctly sized timber processed before treatment or a documented manufacturer-approved detail/product satisfying applicable requirements. Do not simply cut standard pile stock low on site.

Public BRANZ summaries describe standard anchor footings at least900 mm below cleared ground, plan dimensions governed by tables and concrete at least100 mm beneath timber. Those general statements do not specify these project footings or validate the custom tread head. Full current-standard and manufacturer verification remains open; direct BRANZ article/PDF retrieval returned403 in this session. No old400×900/450 schedules are reissued as instructions.

Selected surfaces are preserved. The resulting nominal250 mm rises have not been established as compliant access. The no-handrail preference is not a regulatory finding.
