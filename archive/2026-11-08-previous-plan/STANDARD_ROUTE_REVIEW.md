## Standard bolted route actually examined

NZS3604:2011 Figure6.10 provides an anchor-pile-to-bearer connection using anM12 bolt and50×50×3 washers, together with prescribed/rated joist ties. A larger anchor head can put the bearer in a seated side rebate whose lowest cut is at the ordinary bearing level, rather than cutting a narrow neck down below it.

One geometry candidate, **not a purchasing or construction selection**, is a200-square **H5 anchor-grade pile complying withNZS3605**, a90-wide bearer seat, and110mm of timber remaining behind the seat (figure minimum100). At this deck height the lowest seat cut could be+187.25, above the150mm processing limit on unchanged soil. A full-widthM12 bolt example would have about200mm grip;240mm length is a geometric candidate, not an established product/capacity selection. The pile can continue alongside the bearer to its top; the bolt transfers the prescribed horizontal load and the seat carries gravity.

This shows a way the selected500mm height can fit a documented connection concept without100mm ground lowering. **It does not complete the connection:**

- Actual200-square anchor-grade/markedNZS3605 supply is not confirmed. OrdinaryH5 fence/landscape posts cannot silently replace an approved anchor pile. Published suppliers list200-squareH5 timber; that alone does not establish the necessary pile grade.
- The adjacent joists have335mm clear between them. A200mm head leaves only67.5mm each side: the complete prescribed6kN-per-side joist connection must fit and develop its capacity. Do not transfer the old12KNH kit rating to an altered lower joint or guess which cleats can be omitted.
- Confirm the exact bolt, washer, all edge/end distances, both-ply participation, footings and overall bracing under the applicable standard. No new support count, concrete volume or material order is released from this geometry screen.
