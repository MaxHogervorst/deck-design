Pinepac's house-pile instructions require drying/preservative treatment after processing and no cutting for fixings or other purposes within150 mm of finished ground. The proposed lowest11 mm bolt bore centredZ126 extends toZ120.5. Merely placing its centre above150 is insufficient: the complete cut and applicable installation detail must clear the ground.

For illustration, if actual finished soil were atZ−60, the lowest bore would be180.5 above it and the bottom of a50 mm washer atZ101 would be161 above it. Post top216 would be276 above that soil. The paired footing and fixing detail must establish actual finished/cleared ground, drainage and processing conditions together. There is no assumed125 mm excavation requirement. No change to the selected tread surface is made.

## 7. What needs one specific structural check before release

- Paired-footing soil bearing, lateral/overturning resistance, concrete geometry, reinforcement if needed and inside-footprint construction tolerance.
- Side-bolted rail/post connection capacity and timber end/edge/group rules; current140×45 rail load cases and supported rail ends.
- Four corner connections and four doubled-support connection locations, including fastener clashes and wet-service durability.
- Actual ground levels/drainage and pile manufacturer's processing instructions.

A suitably qualified NZ structural designer needs to check these items before this becomes a dig/drill schedule. A drawing whose parts fit is not evidence that its joints or footing are strong enough. No third party has been contacted and no inspections during construction are assumed.
