## 2. Tread: ordinary box, short boards, concrete supports

**Working selection:** retain the 6600 ×400 mm box and +250 mm pine surface. Two doubled140 ×45 SG8 rails occupy local y0–90 and310–400. Choose minimum H4 treatment for the low frame; Cypress's listed **H5 SG8** is the suggested procurement route. Keep the front rail exposed, natural pine, without added fascia or stain.

Run the32 mm pine boards **front-to-back, cut400 mm long**, directly over the two90 mm-wide rails. There is220 mm clear space /310 mm between rail centrelines. This retains the ordinary single-layer box while avoiding load-bearing short-joist end joints or individual hangers. Board ends bear on the rails; no overhang is needed. User's permitted50 mm overhang is a maximum allowance, not a requirement.

Use **19 ×220 mm crosspieces in90 ×45 SG8**, minimum H4/H5, **bottom-flush** with the rails. Their tops are50 mm below the decking underside, so walking load goes into the rails. Two100 ×3.75 stainless end nails per crosspiece end; connect into the inner rail ply before adding the outer ply. Keep the reviewed two-row90 ×4 stainless ply-stitching schedule. Crosspiece X centres:22.5 +380i for i0…17, plus6577.5. Remove the former two tread board-joint sisters: short boards have no mid-length seam.

The2.7 kN stair point over310 mm produces77.5% of the simple bending demand of1.8 kN over600 mm in the same32 mm board. Compared with the standard19 mm/450 mm decking demand, the corresponding bending/shear demand ratios are0.364/0.891. These are transparent demand comparisons, **not an assigned grade or a manufacturer's stair approval for unidentified merchant boards**. Select sound boards, follow supplier fixing/gap instructions and include actual board suitability in the pre-build check. A simple conservative1.2 m rail-span point model gives1.391 kNm ultimate moment; full built-up rail continuity/stability remains part of the structural detail.

### Results and honest scope

Assume100 kPa allowable soil bearing,24 kN/m³ concrete,0.6 kPa timber dead allowance, and a maximum1.1 m tributary length. Checks exclude favourable soil-cover/passive resistance.

The wind envelope is an assumption, **not a calculated site wind action**. The1.5/1.2 reaction allowances are stated sensitivities, not a complete continuous-pad reaction envelope. Multiplying conservative concrete edge/spacing factors is a component screen, not the complete ETA multi-anchor/group check. **Bracket bend/prying, timber bolt group/splitting, anchor shear/tension interaction, pad resistance and all applicable load combinations remain one bounded structural assembly check for a suitably qualified NZ designer/manufacturer.** Pryda publishes component geometry and leaves assembly capacity to design. Do not buy or drill this proposed restraint as an approved kit.
