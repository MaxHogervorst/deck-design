# Tread and landscaping — clarified owner brief

8 November2026, Auckland. This supersedes every earlier instruction that all tread excavation/concrete must remain inside the400 mm walking-surface footprint. No website change or publication is made in this pass.

## Confirmed by owner

- Underground footings may extend outside the visible tread outline beneath adjacent landscaping.
- The front of the tread will meet a mixture of path and grass/garden. The priority is a neat visible finish, without exposed concrete projections.
- The owner is willing to change details to achieve that finish. No new tread width, depth or height has been selected: retain6600×400 mm and+250 surface as working dimensions.
- H5 timber posts concreted into excavated holes remain the foundation method for both deck and tread. No alternative foundation comparison is introduced.
- A conventional timber box/ladder tread remains the design baseline. The two-wide-bearer substitute was not established as better. The owner's earlier acceptance of50 mm board overhang is permission, not a requirement to retain short front-to-back boards or that substitute frame.
- Lateral connection to the freestanding deck is permitted; its resistance/load path still needs checking. The house supplies no support.

## Appearance and ground-detail objectives

1. Keep the visible walking tread at the selected depth; buried concrete need not follow that outline.
2. Position posts and their concrete heads discreetly behind the tread face. Develop a simple recessed timber fascia to screen framing/hardware while leaving a drainage/ventilation gap. Final board/treatment and dimensions remain to detail.
3. Let the wider footing body extend below the path, grass or garden where needed, with the concrete profile/cover coordinated with path construction and the lawn root zone. Do not promise grass will grow over a shallow concrete shoulder or leave a concrete bump in the path.
4. Keep finished soil, mulch and paving clear of framing and of protected pile cuts/connections as required by the supplied products and applicable details. Concealment is not achieved by banking soil against framing or burying pile-head fixings. Provide drainage away from timber; do not form a water-retaining recess.

These are finish objectives, not a poured-concrete profile or a completed drainage design. Where a local concrete crown remains needed for drainage, conceal it beneath the tread rather than assuming it can be covered at the pile with soil.

## Consequences for design development

- Footing sizes/positions can now be chosen for structural performance and buildability rather than solely for containment within a400 mm strip.
- The former14-post /200-square-footing staggered layout and0.1754375 m³ net takeoff were developed around the old restriction. **Do not carry them forward as the current recommendation or order quantities.** Derive the ordinary box's support arrangement afresh.
- Relaxing the outline restriction does not by itself prove post bearing, solve close-row footing overlap, establish soil capacity or verify the tread/deck ties. Those details still need a dimensioned check.
- The existing low-clearance arithmetic remains relevant:90 mm framing under32 mm decking gives underside+128 and nominal pile top+126 with2 mm DPC. Actual finished ground, product processing requirements and drainage still govern. No blanket ground-lowering depth is selected by this clarification.
- Keep one pre-build discussion followed by one owner build sequence, with no builder inspection visits during construction. No new budget or foundation quantities are issued.

## Basic box-support geometry check — illustration only

A400 mm framing envelope can contain125-square posts with depth centres62.5 /337.5 mm. Each post then supports an outer45 mm rail directly; it can also seat a90 mm-wide doubled rail at the corresponding edge. With an illustrative300 mm-diameter footing, the underground projection is87.5 mm past the nearest tread edge. Staggering a pair225 mm along the frontage gives a355.3 mm centre distance, leaving55.3 mm between those circular hole envelopes.

This verifies that the former visible-outline restriction was forcing unnecessary containment geometry. It is **not** a selected footing diameter, frame depth, post count or structural check. The final visible fascia/nosing offsets must also be included: a fascia thickness must not be silently added to a supposedly fixed finished dimension. Footing crowns/cover, post processing clearance, load capacity and soil remain separate details.
