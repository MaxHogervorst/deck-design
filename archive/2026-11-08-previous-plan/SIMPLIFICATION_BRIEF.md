# Simplification brief — buildable and economical

8 November2026, Auckland. Owner authorises replacing the suggested H5-in-concrete tread foundation if a simpler practical solution exists. Keep the selected deck/tread heights and minimise finishing work. This is the current decision brief, not a selected new footing schedule.

## Fixed intent

- Main6600×2600, rebuilt900×1970 and tread6600×400 remain the working outlines.
- Surfaces about500 /250 mm above garden are retained. No riser-height discussion is reopened by the foundation change.
- Main/rebuilt use conventional timber deck development; H5 pile foundations there remain the economical working method unless a demonstrated improvement warrants a change.
- The low tread may use a different support material/system. H5 posts in concrete are no longer a binding requirement.
- Buried foundations may extend beneath the adjacent path/grass/garden. Appearance matters; do not force hidden foundations to match the walking-surface outline.
- One pre-build builder discussion and one owner sequence; no builder inspection visits during construction.

## Finish selection

Where timber is retained, use a natural treated-pine appearance and only the fascia necessary to tidy the visible front/ends. Keep drainage/ventilation and timber clearances. Do not add decorative cladding, coatings or extensive enclosed skirting by default. Required cut-end preservation remains part of timber installation, not an optional decorative finish.

If the owner accepts a concrete tread surface, develop a plain broom-finished tread with formed front/ends. A suitable non-slip texture, drainage and crack-control/curing details remain necessary; no decorative surface treatment is selected.

Pad/anchor dimensions and capacities must be completed before construction; no new concrete volume or order is released.

## Withdrawal of old development assumptions

Fourteen tread piles, compact200-square footing envelopes, wide90×140 tread bearers, bespoke drop-cleats and all associated quantities are not mandatory or approved solutions. Do not recycle them into the new approach. The artificial400 mm underground-envelope restriction and H5-only rule are both withdrawn by the owner.
